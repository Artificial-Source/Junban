## User-experience notes

- Provider setup, model discovery, history, focused-task entry, approvals, cancellation, and disabled-state recovery are understandable without developer tools.
- Mutation proposals must expose the complete approved payload. The schedule apply card therefore lists every block, including the final block and task identity, in a bounded scroll region rather than truncating generic JSON.
- Local model downloads are deliberately explicit and potentially large; the UI keeps consent, progress, cache state, retry, and removal visible.
- Backup/restore correctly treats cloud credentials as private sidecar state rather than backup content; AI returns disabled after restore instead of implying a usable credential.

## Final disposition

The exact optimized-build browser reruns passed and all four dogfood findings are closed with focused regressions. The separate real-browser local-voice acceptance also passed on the same integrated tree. Phase acceptance still depends on the authoritative disabled and enabled performance runs, final documentation/ledger closure, and the phase gate; this report does not claim those separately required outcomes.
