## Security gate (post-Wave 1)

Required by `P5-PLAN-001`. Scope:

- credential persistence (strict versioned private file, atomic replace, fail-closed load)
- one-time secret output (`--write-token`, no argv/stdout/JSON secret)
- authenticated target selection and redirect rejection
- principal derivation (operator vs automation)
- exhaustive route allow/deny matrix and pre-body authorization
