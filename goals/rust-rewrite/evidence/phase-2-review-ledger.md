## Dogfood, temporal and performance findings

The six `ISSUE-001`–`ISSUE-006` browser/recovery findings are all **fixed** with reproduction and retest evidence in `../../../dogfood-output/phase-2/report.md`. Two additional evidence findings were fixed before closure:

- `P2-TIME-001` — **fixed**. Inbox recent-completion filtering now samples one local civil date and passes exact half-open UTC timestamp bounds; positive/negative-offset and cross-midnight storage tests prevent UTC-date truncation regressions.
- `P2-PERF-001` — **fixed**. SQLite's default ~4 MiB auto-checkpoint caused periodic 400–600 ms bulk/reorder outliers. A tested 250-page (~1 MiB) checkpoint/journal bound reduced authoritative bulk/reorder p95 to 12.35 ms without adding a pool or weakening durability.
